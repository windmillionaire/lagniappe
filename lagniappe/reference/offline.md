When you lose the connection, documents keep your edits in your browser. Forms that work offline let you submit changes to send later.

## Documents

Keep typing in an open document as usual. Your edits stay in this browser and are sent automatically when the connection returns.

## Forms

Fill in the form and press its usual submit button. If the button says **Queued Sync**, your changes are saved in this browser and will send when you reconnect. New tasks and notes show **Pending sync** instead. Typing into a form alone does not save it.

If a form needs the server, its submit button is disabled and says **Server Offline**. You can submit it once the connection returns. This can also happen briefly while the server wakes after inactivity.

## When you reconnect

Documents send their edits automatically, and queued forms send in order. If your form edits conflict with other changes, Lagniappe asks which values to keep. Later submissions wait until you choose.

Do not clear browser storage before your offline work has been sent. Clearing it removes the copies waiting in your browser.

## Cached pages and installation

You can reopen pages available in your browser's cache while offline. They refresh after reconnecting. Where your browser supports it, add Lagniappe to your home screen or install it as an app for convenient access.
