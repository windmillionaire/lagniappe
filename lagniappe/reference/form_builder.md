The Form Builder lets you assemble, preview, and update a Page Form or Task Form in a live draft before saving changes to existing records.

Changes remain a draft until you save the form. Form editing runs on desktop viewports—if you open a form on mobile, you'll see an **Editing is desktop-only** notice with shortcuts **Back to Forms** or **Go to Home**.

## Designing, previewing, and saving

- **Header controls**: Use the **Forms /** breadcrumb to return to the index, click the title to rename inline, open **Form actions** to **Copy Form** or **Delete Form**, step backward/forward with **Undo** / **Redo**, toggle **Preview**, watch the **Saved** / **Save** / **Saving…** pill, or open the **Form settings** gear (**Generate** with AI and **Restrict Access**).
- **Building the layout**: Drag fields from **Components** into the form canvas (or generate a draft when AI is available), then select any element to configure its label and rules in Settings.
- **Testing before saving**: Toggle **Preview** to test required fields, table rows, and conditional visibility without touching real submissions, then click **Save** to publish.

Put guidance people need while typing in field labels or Document/Text content rather than placeholders alone, since placeholders disappear on input. Renaming a label keeps existing answers intact; replacing or deleting elements affects attached pages or tasks—see [Converting and Deleting Form Elements](/help/form_element_changes). While background answer migrations run, Save is disabled and affected submissions lock until the builder reports **Form update finished.** (or **Form update needs attention** with **Retry** if a step fails).
