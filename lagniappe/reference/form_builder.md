The Form Builder defines a page or task form's fields, order, and behavior. Builder edits remain a draft until you save the form.

## Build and review

1. Drag components from **Components** into the form area, or generate a draft from a description when AI generation is available.
2. Select a component to edit its settings and move it into the desired order.
3. Use **Preview** to try the form, including conditional fields and required answers.
4. Save the form to publish the changes.

Use short, specific labels and group related questions together. Instructions that people need after typing belong in labels or explanatory content, rather than only in placeholders.

Changing a form already in use can affect many pages and tasks. Renaming a label keeps its answers. Replacing a component can convert its answers, and deleting it removes them. [Converting and Deleting Form Elements](/help/form_element_changes) explains those choices.

While existing answers are being updated, the builder disables Save and pauses editing of affected submissions. It shows **Form update finished.** when done. If it shows **Form update needs attention**, the error appears beside that message. **Retry** continues the unfinished work and keeps answers already updated.
