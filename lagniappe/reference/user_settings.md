User Settings manages the account attached to a personal page. Available controls depend on whether you are viewing your own account and whether you are authorized to manage the target user.

## Personal preferences

On your own account, use the available profile and sign-out controls. Managed users choose no notification email, five-minute email, or an 8:00 AM local-time digest. Public users instead have a site-email consent setting.

The Owner's own settings separately enable messages/mentions and task assignments from managed users. These choices do not reduce Owner access to workspace content.

## Account administration

Administrators can manage ordinary users' groups and AI access. Only the Owner can manage privileged Administrator accounts. Authorized administrators can change another ordinary user's email; you cannot change your own email here, and the Owner's configured email is not editable through these controls.

**None**, **Ask**, and **Create** govern calls to Lagniappe's configured AI provider. Create includes Ask. Even the Owner's provider tier can be reduced without removing Owner permissions.

Managed users can use enabled external-agent connections regardless of provider tier. Those connections use the person's own AI client and retain ordinary workspace permissions. Manage keys and connections through the available External agent API and AI connection controls; do not share credentials in help requests.
