Public Permissions define the limited categories, projects, and pages visible to signed-in public user accounts—distinct from anonymous visitors reading a published page document.

When an installation allows public sign-ins, public users receive their own limited policy instead of normal managed-user group grants. On **Navigation → Users**, toggle **Show public users** in the header to view public accounts separately from regular managed accounts.

## Managing the Public policy

1. Open **Navigation → Users → User Groups** and choose **Public**.
2. Select which categories, projects, and pages signed-in public users may view or interact with. Notice these choices apply across all public accounts.
3. Promote a person later by converting or adopting them as a managed account with ordinary user group memberships—at that point their normal group and individual permissions replace the public policy.

Public accounts do not share managed-user collaboration features or external AI connections. Likewise, publishing a page document via [Document Settings](/help/document_settings) shares only that document publicly without adding the page's private tasks, form answers, or files to signed-in public permissions.
