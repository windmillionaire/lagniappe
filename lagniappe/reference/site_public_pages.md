title: Public Pages and Search Discovery
related:

---
Public pages have shareable URLs. Site-wide search discovery is an additional setting that controls whether eligible published pages can appear in the public directory and sitemap.

Enable discovery in Admin when the installation should expose that public catalog. Each page can still opt out through its own Public settings. Only explicitly published metadata and selected category labels belong in the catalog; private records and unrelated category memberships are excluded.

Turning discovery off updates the site's crawler instructions and discovery output. It does not make a public page private or revoke its shareable URL. Use the page's visibility control for that.

On a page, **Public Page** shows its sharing link and the **Allow this page to appear in search results** checkbox. Use **Update Public Page** to save a change. Search engines and social previews may continue showing older content until they refresh it.

Private workspace search is separate: signed-in users search their permitted records and general help through Lagniappe's header search box.
