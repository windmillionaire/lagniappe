The task creation control on Home creates work on your personal user page. Use it for reminders and follow-up items without opening another page first.

Enter a short action name, then add a description or schedule if useful. The task remains an ordinary Lagniappe task: you can open it to add context, update it, complete it, and inspect its history.

## Home and due dates

Home emphasizes work that is due soon, including tasks due within the next week. A task scheduled farther ahead may disappear from the immediate list until it becomes relevant; it still exists on its page.

Use the postponement controls to move a reminder to another offered day, or open the task to set a more specific due date or repeating schedule. Completing a scheduled task can arrange its next occurrence.

Tasks belonging to another subject should usually be created on that subject's page. Assignment can bring someone else's task into your work without moving its origin to your personal page.
