Capture personal reminders directly on Home so they live on your personal My Page without cluttering shared workspace pages.

Tasks created from Home belong to **My Page** (`View on My Page`). Each one is an ordinary Lagniappe task—you can open it anytime to add details, attach a form, change its schedule, complete it, or review submission history.

## Home focus and due dates

- **Focused timeframe**: The Home **Tasks** panel highlights work coming up soon, including tasks due **within the next week**. A task scheduled farther in the future disappears from the immediate Home card until it gets closer, while remaining visible on **My Page**.
- **Snoozing in place**: Use **Change due date** controls on Home to postpone a reminder to another offered day, or open the task to pick an exact due date or repeating schedule.
- **Next occurrences**: Completing a scheduled task clears or advances it to its next due date according to its schedule—see [Task Scheduling](/help/task_scheduling).

## Shared vs. personal work

- Work tied to a shared topic belongs on that subject's own page so teammates with access see it in context.
- Being assigned a task on a shared page surfaces that task in your work without moving its origin to **My Page**. Because **My Page** is private to you, standard page **Restrictions** controls are hidden there.
