Capture lightweight text and photo notes on Home or attached directly beneath a page title.

Notes have no separate title field—just a short composer (**Write a note...**) with optional photo attachment and visibility controls.

## Adding a note

- **On Home**: Open the **Notes** panel and use **+** to open the **New Note** composer (`Add Note`), even while offline (**Pending sync** until reconnected).
- **On a Page**: Choose **Page actions → Add note** when you want the note to live beneath that page's title.
- **Adding a photo**: Click **Add photo** to attach an image, or include text, a photo, or both (`Remove` clears a selected image before submitting).

## Who can see a note

Every note asks **Who can see this?**:

- **Only I can see this**: Visible only to you and application Administrators.
- **Show for everyone**: On a page, visible to anyone who can view that page. On Home, this option appears when you are permitted to edit the site.

## Notes vs. Documents and Search

Notes are quick updates attached to Home or a Page; they are not indexed in workspace Search. Use [Documents](/help/documents) or [Pages and Page Information](/help/page_info) for full-text searchable content.
