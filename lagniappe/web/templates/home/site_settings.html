    Keep the installer mailbox active

    Authentication email still uses the historical installer as its sender or SMTP login. Reconfigure email with a durable business-controlled identity using ./setup.sh email and deploy before suspending or deleting that Workspace account.

    What setup handoff manages

    It transfers Lagniappe’s managed bucket and runtime service-account roles, deploys the permanent Owner configuration, closes installer bootstrap, and removes the installer’s direct project IAM binding last.

    What it cannot prove or remove

    Workspace accounts; billing and organization IAM; the installer’s account access to Redis Cloud, Cloudflare or DNS, Resend, or another mail provider; copies of configuration backups or exported archives stored outside Lagniappe’s managed Google Cloud buckets; and credentials on the installer’s computer require separate verification and cleanup.

    Lagniappe deliberately cannot change project IAM from this screen. The application configuration can report its saved handoff state, but the Owner must confirm the historical installer is absent in Google Cloud IAM before treating cloud access removal as complete.
