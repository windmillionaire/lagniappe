Public pages always have shareable links. Search discovery is an additional site-wide choice.

When discovery is enabled, eligible pages appear in the sitemap and may be indexed by search engines. A page can opt out in its Public settings.

{% call callout("warning") %} Turning discovery off updates crawler instructions immediately, but search engines and third-party previews may take time to refresh cached results. {% endcall %} {% endcall %}
