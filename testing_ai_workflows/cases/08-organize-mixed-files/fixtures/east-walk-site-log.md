# East walk drainage project — consolidated site log

Project code: MC-SW-26-041

This export combines field observations, meeting minutes, correspondence notes,
budget drafts, and review comments for a proposed drainage improvement near the
east walk. Dates identify when observations were recorded. They do not, by
themselves, establish purchase, delivery, installation, construction, or
completion.
